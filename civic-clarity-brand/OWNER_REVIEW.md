## Honest limitations

- **2.5D only.** No true 3D geometry exists. Parallax comes from per-tier offsets and a camera push.
- The procedural sky and architecture look clean and illustrative. They do not match the reference's painterly, photographic density.
- **Audio has not been heard by a human.** It was validated only by measurement: loudness, peaks, waveform and spectrum. A listening check on studio monitors and on phone speakers is required.
- Thumbnail templates use placeholder headlines. Real films need verified imagery dropped into `public/` via `imageSrc`.
- **Rights items for you:** a trademark search for the name, Remotion licence eligibility, and the ownership statement for the AI-assisted work (see ASSET_PROVENANCE.csv).
