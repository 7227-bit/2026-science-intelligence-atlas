# PROPOSED Production Bible Amendment — Studio Identity & Ident

> **NOT APPLIED.** This text is a proposal for the owner. It has not been written to the canonical Production Bible or to any Google Drive file. Apply it only after the owner explicitly approves, and then only via the owner's normal governance process.

**Proposed section title:** *Studio Identity, Ident and Evidence Graphics*
**Proposed status line:** "Adopted on [DATE] by [OWNER] — supersedes Brand Master v1.0 prototypes."

## A1. Studio identity

1. The studio wordmark is **CIVIC CLARITY** in Cinzel small capitals with enlarged initials. It is supplied as outlined SVG masters in `civic-clarity-brand/deliverables/logo/svg/`. Never retype it in another font.
2. The optional descriptor is **INVESTIGATIVE DOCUMENTARIES** in Montserrat Medium.
3. The compact mark (C + star in a ring) is the only small-format logo, for avatars, watermarks and favicons. The Capitol is cinematic context only and is never required in the small-format logo.
4. Colours come only from `tokens.json`. The flat master is canonical. The metallic treatment is reserved for cinematic and hero uses.
5. Independence statement: architectural and national motifs are visual context. No seal-like devices. No language or imagery implying government affiliation or endorsement.

## A2. Ident placement

1. The ident is **optional**. When used, it plays **after** the cold-open hook. It never precedes it.
2. The default is 4.5 s. Allowed range is 3.5–6 s via `durationInSeconds`. Omit it from Shorts, from pieces under about 3 minutes, and wherever pacing suffers.
3. Use the silent version or a muted render when the film's score continues through the ident.
4. **Locked material:** no ident, title card or brand element may be inserted into, or retimed within, any approved locked section. In particular, the AI legislation documentary is locked from 0 to 79.8 s. An alternate opening is a separate candidate until the owner explicitly approves it.

## A3. Sonic signature

1. Only the original cue `civic-clarity-sonic-ident.wav` (generated by `scripts/synth-sonic-ident.mjs`) or a version re-derived from it.
2. **Delivery:** −17 LUFS ±1 integrated, ≤ −1 dBTP.
3. No commercial studio stings or library "trailer hits" may be layered with it.

## A4. Evidence graphics

1. Every on-screen source uses `CivicClarityEvidenceCard` with a visible type label: Primary source / Secondary analysis / Civic Clarity analysis / Illustrative reconstruction.
2. Quoted excerpts must be verbatim, with title, publisher, date and locator filled. Placeholders must never ship.
3. Never simulate documents, stamps, redactions, seals or letterheads.
4. Reconstructions and AI-assisted imagery must carry the *Illustrative reconstruction* label on screen.

## A5. Thumbnails

1. Use template A (Institution) or B (Evidence). Per-film imagery must be verified and cleared.
2. The vector Capitol key art is a fallback and must not repeat across consecutive uploads.
3. Headlines run 2–5 words with one accent word. Keep the timestamp zone clear.

## A6. QA gate

Before any brand asset is published, `npm run qa` must pass. The asset must also have an entry in `ASSET_PROVENANCE.csv` with a permission status other than UNKNOWN.

## A7. Open items the owner must resolve before adoption

- Trademark clearance search for "Civic Clarity".
- Remotion licence eligibility (Free vs Company licence).
- Final typeface decision: Cinzel, or a commercially licensed alternative.
- Ownership statement for the AI-assisted synthesised cue and the procedural artwork.
